About the club

Training support, community, and structure for every member.

Uranus Fitness is a community-focused workshop and club dedicated to improving strength, mobility, and overall wellbeing through accessible classes and peer-led training sessions. We welcome participants of all experience levels.

What we offer

A clear, well-spaced overview of the workshop experience

Programs and sessions

  • Weekly instructor-led workshops covering fundamentals and advanced techniques.
  • Open gym sessions for members to practice and socialize.
  • Special events and guest instructors throughout the year.

Workshop structure

  1. Warm-up and mobility drills (10–15 minutes)
  2. Technique instruction and partner drills (20–30 minutes)
  3. Conditioning or skill practice (15–25 minutes)
  4. Cool-down and Q&A (5–10 minutes)

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Anyone can join — we welcome complete beginners through to experienced athletes. There are no prerequisites or fitness tests. All sessions are structured so that participants work at their own pace.

Complete the registration form on this site. Once submitted, you will receive a confirmation and details for your first session. Walk-ins are also accepted subject to space availability.

Wear comfortable athletic clothing and bring a water bottle. Most equipment is provided. If you have personal gear (lifting belt, straps, etc.) you are welcome to bring it, but it is not required.

Instructor-led workshops run weekly. Open gym sessions are available on additional days. Check the schedule page for current times and any special events.

Fee details are provided during registration. Some sessions are free for enrolled students; others have a small cost to cover facility access. Contact us at info@uranusfitness.example for specifics.
